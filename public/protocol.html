Keep three amounts separate.

  • In progressA verified payment is still being completed.
  • SpentPayment settlement is confirmed.

Technical names and refund limits

Reserved + committed + spent = exposure. Amounts use integer lovelace. Verified delivery and payment settlement are separate facts. A refund observation does not automatically prove full fee-inclusive recovery.
