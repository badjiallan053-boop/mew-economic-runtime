THREE DIFFERENT COSTS

Paying an editor is not buying an ad.

✂

Production

The editing job: five files, one revision allowance and a fixed quote. Record which exact work the customer accepts.

Demonstrated with synthetic outline commitments.
↗

Advertising

The distribution budget: account, audience, dates and spending limit. A clip approval does not authorize launching an ad.

Separate approval and ad-platform connection needed.
◎

Creator compensation

A creator’s fee or performance bonus. Agree the metric, reporting window, cap and dispute rules before making a promise.

Future contract. Views do not authorize a payout.

Keep currencies separate. The current demo accounts in ADA; it does not combine dollar ad spend, ADA and USDM, or enforce one budget across separate campaigns.

SAME WORKFLOW / DIFFERENT RAILS

Useful for Web2.
A payment path for Web3.

Web2 · proposed pilot

A software launch

A company turns its product webinar into five clips. A human approves the files, publishes them and compares demo signups with its usual campaign.

  • Source: customer-owned recording
  • Delivery: private file storage and review
  • Measurement: platform export + consented website events
  • Payment: existing invoices handled by the team

Start manually. A future fiat payment adapter needs its own accounting and verification. It is not connected today.

Web3 · proposed pilot

A Cardano product demo

A builder turns a walkthrough into five educational clips. Review the product claims, accept the files, then test a bounded supplier payment on preprod.

  • Source: approved product walkthrough
  • Delivery: exact artifact + authenticated receipt
  • Measurement: views and signups, measured separately
  • Payment: prepared Cardano escrow path

Private database access, signer custody, funding and independent audit remain pending. No transaction has been signed or broadcast.

MEASURE THE RIGHT THING

Attention is a signal.
It isn’t a sale.

Did people watch?Views · watch time · retention
→
Did people act?Tracked visits · signups · qualified leads
→
Was it worth the cost?Accepted assets · observed cost · customer outcomes

Link the campaign ID to each clip version, published post and reporting window. Keep missing observations marked unknown. Compare with a baseline before claiming an improvement; a tracked signup alone does not prove the clip caused it.

Read-only analytics are a proposed connection. The rehearsal’s 3,000 views are an invented fixture, not campaign performance. YouTube’s metric definitions explain why report context matters.
