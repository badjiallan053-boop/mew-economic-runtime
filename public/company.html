    Critical handoffs

    1. Founder approves the mandate and acceptance criteria.
    2. Knowledge and research supply evidence; procurement proposes options.
    3. Risk and red team review independently. Rejection or missing evidence blocks downstream tasks.
    4. Engineering, QA and delivery assess changes and artifacts.
    5. Finance reports funding gaps. Operations prepares recovery. Communications explains only supported claims.

    Skills and external sources are reference data. They cannot grant tools, change budgets or authorize payments.

    Open design studio

    One marketing workflow. Clear owners.

    These responsibilities map to the existing team; they describe a proposed pilot, not active model workers.

    1. Plan — chief of staff + knowledge: recording ID, source rights, audience and one five-outline brief.
    2. Produce — procurement + engineering: one supplier request, source time ranges and a versioned outline bundle. A timeout keeps the first request occupied.
    3. Review — delivery + risk + red team: inspect the exact bundle and original source independently. Acceptance cannot be inherited from the producer.
    4. Measure — research + finance + communications: link approved files to later posts and customer actions. Report production cost, ad spend and creator fees separately.

    Current rehearsal stops at synthetic outlines and fixture evidence. Real videos, account connections, distribution and conversion data require a separate approved integration.
