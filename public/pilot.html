Can we use this with a live wallet today?

Payment execution is disabled. A real preprod job needs asset-aware accounting, approved custody, dispatch review and independent chain and collection evidence.

Does the demo use real models or generate video?

The hosted demo uses deterministic fixtures. The clip rehearsal produces outlines, not rendered clips. Live model evaluation is a separate pending gate.

What is the next step after downloading?

Review the scope with your team. Assign acceptance and support owners, agree rights and budgets, then arrange evaluation through an existing founder contact. No contact channel or pilot booking service is connected here.
