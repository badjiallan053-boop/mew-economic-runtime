THREE DIFFERENT JOBS

Control the action. Record the facts. Decide coverage separately.

  1. 01

    Operator sets authority

    Which agent, tools, actions, recipient, asset and limit are allowed?

    Owned by the deploying organization
  2. 02

    MEW records execution

    Was the action allowed, reserved, uncertain, verified, delivered or reversed?

    Deterministic control and evidence
  3. 03

    Insurer evaluates risk

    Do the facts matter under this application, policy wording and claim?

    Owned by the authorized insurance partner

Key boundary: MEW’s ALLOW means “within this configured mandate.” It does not mean a broker accepts the risk, a policy covers an event, or a claim will be paid.

ONE WORKFLOW · FOUR SOURCES OF TRUTH

Each participant proves its own part.

MEW connects the records. It does not take over the authority of the operator, wallet, supplier or insurer.

  1. 01 · CUSTOMER

    Operator mandate

    Declares the agent, allowed action, recipient and limit.

    Authority: customer
  2. 02 · CONTROL

    MEW decision

    Reserves capacity and records ALLOW, DEFER or DENY.

    Evidence: MEW journal
  3. 03 · RAIL

    Cardano observation

    Reports a transaction and output at a measured confirmation depth.

    Evidence: chain observer
  4. 04 · SERVICE

    Supplier delivery

    Confirms the exact job and delivered artifact.

    Evidence: supplier receipt
  5. 05 · REVIEW

    Partner decision

    Broker, insurer or claims team makes its own determination.

    Authority: licensed partner

Never merge these events: a transaction hash does not prove delivery; a delivery receipt does not prove settlement; an MEW decision does not establish insurance coverage.

Current status: the website is a demo, Cardano support is a preprod observation and unsigned-escrow prototype, and the insurance evidence pack is proposed. No live signing, payment, policy or claim decision is available.

THE ASSURANCE LAYER · ONE OPERATION ID FROM START TO FINISH

Eight checks keep one agent payment accountable.

In the target design, one operation ID follows a request across the agent, MEW, payment rail and supplier. Each checkpoint names who can attest to it and what happens when evidence is missing.

  1. 01 · AUTHORITY

    Who may act?

    Bind the agent, human owner, action class, allowed recipient and mandate revision.

    Operator attests · signed mandate required
  2. 02 · INTENT

    What is the job?

    Give the objective and effect stable IDs; set quantity, purpose and duplicate key.

    MEW checks · same intent reuses its ID
  3. 03 · QUOTE

    What will it cost?

    Bind supplier, service, amount, asset, expiry and recipient before approval.

    Supplier provides · MEW validates terms
  4. 04 · RESERVE

    Is capacity available?

    Check the whole mandate and reserve budget plus quantity in one durable transaction.

    MEW decides · no reserve means no dispatch
  5. 05 · DISPATCH

    Was it sent once?

    Persist an outbox item and stable idempotency key before a worker calls the provider.

    Worker reports · timeout stays unresolved
  6. 06 · SETTLEMENT

    Did value move?

    Verify the exact rail, asset, payer/recipient, amount and transaction against saved terms.

    Rail observer attests · inclusion is not delivery
  7. 07 · DELIVERY

    Was the job fulfilled?

    Verify the enrolled supplier receipt and exact accepted artifact or service result.

    Supplier signs · payment alone is not acceptance
  8. 08 · RECOVERY

    Can we explain the ending?

    Reconcile the original operation after retry, refund, reversal, timeout or restart.

    Operator reviews · unknown keeps capacity held
Across every step: advisory agents can gather and challenge evidence; deterministic code enforces limits; an enrolled signer controls keys; a named human owns exceptions. No agent recommendation can approve its own spend.
WEB2 ADAPTER

Cards · invoices · service APIs

Use provider idempotency, authenticated webhooks and bank/card reconciliation. A timeout triggers lookup of the original payment.

WEB3 ADAPTER

Cardano · escrow · tokens

Bind network, asset unit, UTxOs, fee/collateral and transaction outputs. A confirmation is an observation; a contract needs its own review.

What exists today: the deterministic reserve/defer kernel, durable journals, authenticated delivery receipt path and read-only Cardano observation. Private production identity, signed mandates, a live dispatch worker, fully bound settlement proof and a funded audited lifecycle are still pending.

PROPOSED EVIDENCE PACK

Make the system inspectable before assigning a score.

SYSTEM

What is deployed?

Workflow and version, model/provider where known, connected tools, action scope, accountable owner and change history.

CONTROLS

What limits it?

Per-action and aggregate caps, approval thresholds, tested retries, stop controls and recovery owner.

OUTCOMES

What actually happened?

Stable operation ID, decision, reservation, external reference, verified observation, delivery and resolution—with gaps visible.

A digest helps detect changed evidence bytes; it does not authenticate the underlying facts. No prompts, keys, seed phrases or raw customer content belong in the default pack.

KEEP THE RISK TYPES SEPARATE

An execution error is not a market loss.

A future trading adapter could record duplicate orders, wrong side/size, replay or an exceeded mandate. Price movement, liquidation, oracle failure, MEV, custody loss and protocol exploits need distinct evidence and risk transfer. MEW does not promise to insure any of them.

What this prototype does not do

  • Quote, bind or administer insurance
  • Set premiums, limits or exclusions
  • Decide liability or adjudicate claims
  • Sign or send trades or payments
  • Predict loss from synthetic demo data
