A FEW CLEAR ANSWERS

Before we
build together.

Explore agent responsibilities ↗
Is MEW a wallet or a payment rail?

MEW tracks a shared purchasing objective and the exposure of related requests. Cardano and Masumi are payment integration paths; this public prototype does not hold wallet keys or execute payments.

Who decides that two requests are equivalent?

The principal defines an explicit semantic key for the purchase. MEW checks that pre-agreed identity. The prototype does not infer equivalence from free text or let a model authorize spending.

Does a timeout free the budget?

No. An unknown outcome retains capacity. The original request must be reconciled with appropriate evidence before another equivalent purchase can proceed.

What would a first pilot establish?

Whether the mandate prevents duplicate requests in your chosen workflow, including retries and uncertain evidence. Live integration also needs authenticated operators, a funded test wallet, validated transaction evidence and independent security review.
