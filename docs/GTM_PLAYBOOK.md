# MEW go-to-market and product writing

Shared context: [PRODUCT_MARKETING_CONTEXT.md](PRODUCT_MARKETING_CONTEXT.md). Implementation assignments: prompts/workstreams/product-writing.md and prompts/workstreams/gtm.md. These prompts guide host-side contributors; they are not activated live models.

## Positioning hypothesis

MEW helps agent builders inspect whether another deliverable purchase fits a shared quantity and budget mandate when the first outcome remains uncertain. The buyer is initially a technical founder or workflow owner procuring reports or other accepted artifacts through agents. This audience and its willingness to pay remain unvalidated.

The secondary hypothesis is a clip-production agency coordinating suppliers and revisions. Validate its distinct procurement problem before expanding the offer. Current clip outputs are synthetic outlines, not rendered videos.

The current product offers an inspectable synthetic decision rehearsal, evidence records and advisory workflow demonstrations. Live models, authenticated external fulfillment and payments remain gated. Cardano and Masumi are an integration path, not evidence of customer demand or endorsed partnerships.

## Differentiation and proof

| Buyer question | Mechanism to inspect | Evidence and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What happens after a supplier timeout? | Unresolved exposure retains mandate capacity. | SPEC.md and the persisted decision rehearsal; synthetic outcome. |
| Can several proposed purchases exceed one objective? | Quantity and integer lovelace exposure are evaluated together. | Core implementation and regression tests; actions bypassing MEW are outside its boundary. |
| What supports the decision? | Checkpoint record separates delivery and settlement. | Demo records; these are not merchant receipts or chain transactions. |
| Can we try our workflow? | A bounded pilot brief defines inputs, acceptance and ownership. | Proposed customer validation; a locally generated brief is not submitted or scheduled. |

Lead with the procurement problem and a concrete demonstration. Explain architecture after the visitor understands the decision. Avoid guaranteed savings, “fully safe” claims, generic AI-company language and unverified partner logos.

## First-value journey

1. Inspect the synthetic timeout scenario.
2. Explain why a second equivalent purchase cannot treat silence as a verified failure.
3. Locate the retained exposure and checkpoint record.
4. Prepare a local pilot brief for one workflow, with an acceptance rubric and a responsible human.

Steps 2 and 3 define the initial activation hypothesis. Merely visiting the page or clicking a button does not demonstrate understanding. Local learning feedback is self-reported; it is not independently verified competence or a centrally collected metric.

## Proposed pilot offer

Offer a scoped workflow review and synthetic rehearsal first: one deliverable type, one mandate, two supplier candidates and agreed acceptance evidence. Produce a reviewed brief documenting the timeout decision and unresolved integration gates. Duration, price, operator availability and execution budget must be agreed before booking or purchasing; none are promised by the website.

Qualification requires a specific recent procurement example, an owner able to approve a pilot, rights-cleared inputs, an acceptance rubric and willingness to review failure cases. Defer requests requiring unattended mainnet spending, undisclosed customer data or guarantees of output quality. A research interview and a customer pilot are different commitments.

## Weekly learning loop

Human research owner: **unassigned**. Human pilot owner: **unassigned**. Human claims/release reviewer: **unassigned**. Advisory writing and GTM agents prepare artifacts; humans decide outreach, commercial commitments and release claims.

Proposed first experiment: invite five qualified agent-workflow owners to consented moderated sessions. Show one message, ask them to complete the timeout rehearsal and explain the record before helping. This sample is a planning target, not an observed cohort or statistically powered comparison.

| Proposed measure | Exact definition | Collection and decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Activation | Eligible consenting participant explains retained capacity and identifies the record without coaching, within the session. | Reviewer records pass/fail and participant wording. If fewer than three of five succeed, revise the explanation before scaling invitations. |
| Problem fit | Participant describes a real unresolved supplier purchase and its current workaround. | Interview notes distinguish recent experience from hypothetical interest. Fewer than two concrete examples triggers a reconsideration of the initial audience. |
| Pilot intent | Qualified participant explicitly agrees to review a scoped brief with an accountable owner. | Record the agreement date; a CTA click or generated brief does not qualify. |
| Time to first value | Time from scenario start until the activation task completes. | Observer timing; report median only with cohort size and failures separately. No benchmark exists yet. |

Review weekly: retain verbatim language with consent, group objections, select one change, and record the next hypothesis. Keep customer notes in an approved private location; public demo state is unsuitable. No visitor analytics or contact collection is implied. Cohort counts and results are currently **not observed**.

## Draft outreach, not sent

**Agent-builder discovery:** “We’re testing MEW, a prototype for purchases coordinated by agents. The demo explores a supplier timeout and a second request for the same report. Have you encountered an unresolved purchase in your workflow? If so, would you review that failure case with us? This is a research conversation; the prototype does not execute payments.”

**Agency discovery:** “We’re exploring how clip-production teams handle uncertain delivery and repeat supplier orders. Could you walk us through one recent example and how you decided whether to order again? Our current rehearsal uses synthetic clip outlines. We’re looking for feedback before designing a real pilot.”

**Ecosystem partner discovery:** “MEW has a synthetic economic-control demo and prepared Cardano preprod verification boundaries. We’d value a technical review of the mandate-to-evidence integration. Live payment proof is pending. Would reviewing one bounded failure case fit your current work?”

These are templates without recipient lists or invented contacts. They are not sent automatically.
