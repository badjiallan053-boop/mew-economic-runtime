# Shared product marketing context

Current positioning hypothesis: MEW is an economic-control prototype for teams building agents that purchase accepted deliverables. It counts equivalent actions against one principal's quantity and exposure mandate, including unresolved outcomes. It governs actions routed through its accounting boundary; it does not deduplicate arbitrary natural-language goals.

Primary buyer hypothesis: technical founder or workflow owner buying reports through agents. Secondary hypothesis: agencies coordinating clip suppliers. No validated customer segment, traction, savings or willingness-to-pay result is available.

Problem example: a supplier's delivery response times out after a commitment. A second agent proposes the same report. The uncertainty does not establish failure or release objective capacity. The visitor can inspect this behavior in the persisted synthetic decision demo.

Website contract: Overview explains the problem; Decision demo exercises fixtures and exposes the record; Pilot brief creates a local proposal without submission or signup; Architecture and Evidence support deeper inspection. No customer data, contact address, booking or analytics integration has been added. A generated pilot brief is not acceptance of a pilot.

First-value hypothesis: the visitor explains retained capacity and finds the record supporting it. The optional local question gives learning feedback; it does not centrally measure activation or prove understanding independently. Use consented observed sessions for the proposed metrics in GTM_PLAYBOOK.md.

Voice: precise, plain and calm. Explain budget and quantity before accounting vocabulary. Prefer “Try decision demo” and “Prepare pilot brief” to vague actions. Keep illustrative ADA amounts distinct from pricing. Describe live models, customer fulfillment and payments as pending. Avoid autonomous-company claims, partner logos, testimonials, savings percentages and guarantees.

Evidence sources: SPEC.md, current source and tests, separately verified deployed asset hashes, and explicitly labeled synthetic fixtures. Source publication and preparation do not establish live model or payment execution.
