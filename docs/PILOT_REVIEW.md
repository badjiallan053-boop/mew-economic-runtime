# Product and marketing pilot review

7 October 2026. Independent review of the company and campaign design. This is a
proposed experiment, not customer validation or proof of operating agents.

## Choose one buyer and outcome

Test the Growth Treasury hypothesis with a small agency or creator team that
already repurposes owned long-form media. The smallest service is one approved
source video converted into five usable promotional clips with a fixed revision
allowance. Compare it with the buyer's current editor workflow. MEW's proposed
value is fewer duplicate commitments and clearer acceptance/recovery, rather
than a promise of more views or an autonomous marketing department.

The current campaign produces synthetic outlines, not clips. A real pilot needs
an authorized editor or one bounded generation adapter, private source storage,
artifact versions, recorded rights and an authenticated acceptance owner. Use
manual operations where practical before automating procurement or publishing.

## Evidence gates and accountable owners

| Gate | Owner | Evidence required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Problem exists | Research + founder | Authorized customer interviews; current time, cost, revisions and failure examples; record disconfirming evidence |
| Work is permitted | Risk + customer | Ownership and music/likeness/brand scope; distinguish organic use from advertising rights |
| Result is usable | Delivery + customer | Exact file/version accepted against duration, aspect ratio, captions, claims and brief; approved revision count |
| Service creates value | Product + Finance | Actual elapsed time, customer review minutes, accepted assets and observed total cost, compared with baseline |
| Service is recoverable | Operations + QA | Source deletion, failed upload, substituted artifact and interrupted supplier-action drills; named support owner |
| Payment is safe | Engineering + Procurement | Immutable quote/effect/asset bindings and reconciliation; uncertainty preserves capacity; delivery is separate from settlement |
| Repeat demand exists | Founder | A second requested mission or paid repeat; compliments and demo attendance do not establish demand |

Set numerical go/change/stop thresholds with the pilot customer before starting;
do not fabricate a baseline or claim that five interviews establish a market.
No outreach, purchase or account access is authorized by this document.

## Overlooked product decisions

- The buyer, media owner, reviewer and advertising-account operator may be four
  different people. A single bearer token does not represent those permissions.
- Decide who absorbs rejected work and revision costs. Define supplier recourse
  and customer support before claiming an accountable service.
- Record source-to-clip lineage and exact approval versions. Digest integrity
  does not authenticate a reviewer or establish legal permission.
- Measure human review and support time alongside model/editor fees. A cheap
  generation can still be an expensive accepted deliverable.
- Keep production, ad spend and performance liabilities in separate typed
  contracts. Current ADA fixtures cannot account for fiat/USDM campaigns.
- Do not optimize impressions until a trustworthy analytics contract exists.
  Attribution windows, account identity and suspicious traffic remain unresolved.

## Mature-team practices worth adopting now

Maintain one outcome backlog with explicit owner, acceptance evidence and release
gate. Run small canaries; record actual deployment revision, rollback and recovery
instructions. Require a privacy/retention decision for media before collection.
Compare a small multi-role workflow with a single-provider/manual baseline. Add
roles only when measured quality or recovery improves enough to justify cost.
Preserve independent risk/red-team inputs; indiscriminate agent cross-talk spreads
untrusted context and can erase reviewer independence.

Cardano/Masumi is a procurement/settlement option, not the customer outcome. The
pilot can demonstrate artifact acceptance first, then add one preprod payment
after credentials, funding and dispatch controls are ready. No signer, live
payment, successful ad campaign or marketplace registration is established here.
