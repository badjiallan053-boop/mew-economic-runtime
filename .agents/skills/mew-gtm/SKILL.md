# MEW go to market

Read docs/PRODUCT_MARKETING_CONTEXT.md and docs/GTM_PLAYBOOK.md and inspect the relevant current page before proposing a funnel. Treat agent builders buying deliverables as the initial customer hypothesis; clip agencies are a secondary hypothesis, not proven demand.

Define a customer job, its current workaround and the decision the experiment will resolve. MEW's useful first experience is understanding why an unresolved purchase retains capacity and locating the supporting record. Opening a page alone is not activation.

Keep each experiment to one audience, one message and one observable task. Specify eligibility, denominator, observation method, completion window, disconfirming result and a named human owner or “unassigned”. Separate proposed metrics from collected observations. Without implemented analytics, use consented moderated sessions and a private research log; do not invent conversion rates or silently collect visitor data.

Qualification asks for a concrete repeat-purchase or supplier-delivery workflow, an acceptance rubric, an owner and rights to the inputs. An interested partner is not a customer. A completed synthetic rehearsal is not a paid pilot. Define commercial terms before any real purchase; this skill grants no spending, outreach, credential or publication authority.

Return a bounded experiment brief, its evidence limitations and the next learning decision. Prefer reusable customer artifacts over extra agent roles. Draft messages when requested; sending requires the user's explicit instruction.
