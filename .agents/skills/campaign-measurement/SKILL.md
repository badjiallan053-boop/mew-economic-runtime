# Campaign measurement procedure

Original local skill. Read AGENTS.md, SPEC.md and docs/MARKETING_CAMPAIGN.md.
This is analysis guidance; it grants no analytics account access, ad spending,
publication, reserve release or performance-payout authority.

1. Record campaign, artifact/version, account, platform, observation source,
   metric definition, timezone, observation time and measurement window.
2. Separate platform-observed counts from attributable outcomes. Never infer
   sales or incremental lift from views. Describe sampling and missing data.
3. Check duplicates, inconsistent account identity, changed metric definitions,
   stale exports, suspicious growth and unverified screenshots. Report unknowns;
   do not invent a fraud verdict or label a count verified without a verifier.
4. Mark open-window observations provisional. A low count today does not show
   that future payable views cannot accrue. Silence and timeout prove neither
   zero results nor release of a financial obligation.
5. Use the immutable contract's caps, measurement cutoff, reconciliation and
   dispute conditions when describing a possible final payable amount. Agents
   recommend only; the trusted economic host admits verified evidence.
6. Keep production commitments, advertising spend and performance compensation
   separately typed. Do not aggregate ADA, USDM and fiat amounts or invent an
   exchange rate. Fees and refunds require their own bound evidence.
7. Report cost per accepted asset and time to acceptance when actually observed.
   Marketing outcome comparisons require a baseline and disclosed attribution
   limits. Simulation task completion is not measured customer value.
8. Recommend the next reversible experiment with a success condition, stopping
   condition and authorized owner. Preserve immutable IDs across retries.
