# MEW partner demo

Read AGENTS.md, docs/INVESTOR_PARTNER_DEMO.md, docs/LIVE_DEPLOYMENT.md and docs/NEXT_PILOT.md. Preserve all runtime contracts and permissions.

1. Choose one audience, one operational problem and one observable proof. Describe reservation, duplicate authorization rejection and retained UNKNOWN exposure in plain language.
2. Classify every claim as hosted observation, tested local implementation, synthetic example or proposed work. Record commit and observation date. Never turn a test count into a security guarantee or traction claim.
3. Demonstrate the timeout case before architecture. Keep exact units and quantities visible. Delivery authenticity, customer acceptance and settlement are separate evidence.
4. Ask a pilot customer for an authorized source asset and acceptance rubric; ask a rail partner for interface review and preprod support. Named organizations are prospective targets, never established partners.
5. Separate infrastructure and clipping hypotheses. Do not invent revenue, customers, market size, deployed model quality, token payments or performance improvements.
6. Finish with a bounded pilot milestone, measurable acceptance conditions and unresolved prerequisites. Do not send outreach or grant tool authority from this skill.
