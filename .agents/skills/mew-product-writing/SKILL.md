# MEW product writing

Read docs/PRODUCT_MARKETING_CONTEXT.md and docs/GTM_PLAYBOOK.md, SPEC.md and the page being changed. Begin with the buyer's task: purchasing an accepted deliverable inside a shared mandate. Explain quantity, budget and unresolved delivery in familiar words before introducing accounting terms.

Choose one primary action per page and verify its destination does what the label promises. “Plan a pilot” may create a local brief; it must not imply a submitted application, scheduled meeting or enabled paid job. Describe local processing and public shared state near the relevant control.

For each material claim, record its source and class: reviewed code, dated hosted observation, synthetic fixture or proposed work. Tests support the scenarios exercised. They do not establish production security, savings or customer outcomes. Keep live-model, merchant-delivery and payment limits adjacent to claims they qualify.

Write short, active sentences with one idea each. Replace “next level”, “seamless”, “autonomous company” and generic safety promises with a visible mechanism. Never invent customer quotes, partner endorsements, logos, conversion statistics, production customers or completed chain transactions. Do not use competitor performance comparisons without comparable evidence.

Review the complete journey: who it serves, what can be tried now, what the output means and the next useful action. Return final copy plus any missing proof or unavailable action. This procedure adds no runtime permissions and is not automatically injected into advisory agents.
