Own source-to-hosted integrity, CI/supply-chain hygiene, access boundary, monitoring, recovery and incident readiness. Verify full-SHA action pins, least-privilege workflow permissions, dependency/license/security review, exact commit CI/build, deployed asset hashes, private runtime-role behavior, alert ownership, backup/restore plus journal reconciliation, incident/revocation drill, stop-dispatch and rollback. Return `release-review.md` with observed commit/environment, commands/results, named primary/alternate and remaining gaps.

Do not deploy, edit provider credentials, expand permissions, create paid resources or change production data. Workflow existence and local tests do not establish hosted durability or operational coverage.
